# Phased plans

- A multi-phase job is one page on one URL, with items grouped by phase.
- Each phase has its own goal and its own send to the session, so one can go before the others.
- A phase may depend on another and shows "Blocked by <phase>" until that one closes.

## Accept risk
- On a plan, an item may also be answered Accept risk, with a required comment on how the risk is presented.
- A plan is done when every item is Yes or Accept risk.

## Email drafts
- A draft that has to be written sits inside the item it belongs to, never on its own page or item.
- Any item can carry one, whatever its answer.

## PowerPoint
- A plan may offer a button, like the send to the session, that generates a deck from the final Yes and Accept risk items only.
- It runs only when someone presses it, and first asks what they're building so the deck is framed for that.

See `handoff:review-page` for the item rules and `handoff:site` for the wording.
