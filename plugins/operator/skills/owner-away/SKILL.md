# Owner away, focus mode, check-ins

- Owner away: when the owner says they are away, ask no questions. Queue every approval and decision in the owner queue ($WARREN_OWNER_QUEUE), grouped by repo, in merge order, with evidence. Keep agents working on anything that needs no decision, and present the whole queue in one batch when the owner is back.
- Focus mode: when a blocker needs undivided attention, hold all other notices, questions and agent reports. Agents keep running, and their results go to the owner queue. When the blocker is fixed, say focus mode is over, present the queue in one batch and resume where the backlog stopped.
- Where the owner is: the owner says when they are out, on the phone or back at the computer; until told otherwise, assume they are at the computer. While something waits on the owner, check in once with a one-line "at your desk, on your phone or out?" after about 2 hours with no reply, and again at each review window.
