# Questions and dialogs

- Don't pop a question dialog while the owner may be typing. When a peer report arrives during typing, or right after a task the owner just gave, say in one line that a question is waiting and ask once the owner is done.
- Ask with selectable options. On a host with an operator session, other sessions send their questions to the operator (see operator:sessions).

## Lessons

- Don't pop a question dialog while the owner may be typing. A dialog can grab keystrokes mid-message.
