# What's next?

- When the owner says this, they are ready to review.
- Say how many items are waiting on them.
- Bring the small wins first, as one batch of up to four quick one-click decisions or merges per question.
- Then bring the medium items, then the big ones last, each with its link and what is needed.
- The review queue empties instead of piling up. Putting the most important item first leaves things that need review behind, and nothing gets done.
- It counts the same as ask, ready or go.
